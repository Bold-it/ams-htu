## 🚦 THE TRAFFIC LIGHT COMPLIANCE SYSTEM
The system automatically categorizes every academic programme based on its proximity to the GTEC expiry date.

| Status | Color | Timeline | Required Action |
|:---:|:---:|---|---|
| **Active** | 🟢 | > 15 Months | Maintain current quality standards. |
| **Upcoming** | 🔵 | 12-15 Months | **Action Required**: Start SAR & Review Curriculum. |
| **Warning** | 🟡 | 3-12 Months | **Renewal Phase**: Submission should be in GTEC's hands. |
| **Critical** | 🔴 | < 90 Days | **Urgent**: Contact QA Unit immediately. |
| **Expired** | ⚫ | 0 Days | **Non-Compliant**: Cease student intake for programme. |

## 👤 USER ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES

### 1. Heads of Department (HoDs)
*   **Monitor**: Check your department's dashboard weekly for **Yellow/Red** alerts.
*   **Evidence**: Upload the latest GTEC certificates to the **Document Vault**.
*   **Prepare**: Complete the **Readiness Checklist** 12 months before expiry.

### 2. Quality Assurance (QA) Officers
*   **Audit**: Review uploaded evidence for authenticity and date accuracy.
*   **Remind**: Dispatch manual "Nudge" emails for Critical programmes.
*   **Report**: Generate monthly compliance summaries for the Pro-VC.

### 3. Management (Pro-Vice-Chancellor)
*   **Oversee**: View institutional compliance percentages on the main dashboard.
*   **Review**: Read the automated **Monthly Audit Report** (sent every 1st of the month).

## 📂 THE DOCUMENT VAULT: BEST PRACTICES

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Single Source of Truth**: The Document Vault is the only recognized repository for accreditation evidence. Physical copies are secondary to the digital record.

*   **File Format**: Prefer **PDF** for certificates and reports.
*   **Naming**: Use a clear format: `[DEPT]_[PROG]_[YEAR]_Certificate.pdf`
*   **Timeliness**: Upload new certificates within **48 hours** of receipt from GTEC.
