## 1.0 INTRODUCTION

The HTU Accreditation Monitoring System is a centralized digital platform designed to ensure Ho Technical University maintains full compliance with the **Ghana Tertiary Education Commission (GTEC)** regulations. This guide provides step-by-step instructions for Departmental Heads (HoDs) and Faculty Officers to monitor, update, and secure accreditation records.

---

## 2.0 SYSTEM ACCESS & ROLES

Access is restricted to authorized university personnel.
- **Departmental Admins**: Can view and edit programmes within their specific department.
- **Viewers**: Read-only access to institutional dashboards.

> [!NOTE]
> All users must sign in using their official **HTU Google Account**. First-time users will require activation from the Quality Assurance Unit.

---

## 3.0 DASHBOARD & COMPLIANCE METRICS

Upon logging in, the **Executive Dashboard** provides a high-level view of institutional health through the "Traffic Light" monitoring system.

### 3.1 Priority Status Definitions
| Status | Meaning | Institutional Requirement |
|:---:|---|---|
| **🟢 Active** | More than 15 months remaining. | Compliant. No immediate action. |
| **🔵 Upcoming** | 12-15 months until expiry. | **Preparation Phase**: Initiate SAR drafting. |
| **🟡 Warning** | 3-12 months remaining. | **Renewal Phase**: Submission should be in progress. |
| **🔴 Critical** | Less than 90 days remaining. | **Urgent**: Immediate intervention required. |
| **⚫ Expired** | Past official expiry date. | **Non-Compliant**: Accreditation is void. |

## 4.0 MANAGING PROGRAMMES

### 4.1 Registering a New Programme
1. Select **Dashboard** -> **Add Accreditation**.
2. Provide the formal Programme Name, Faculty, and Department.
3. Input the **Start Date** and **Expiry Date** exactly as they appear on the GTEC certificate.
4. Define the **Workflow Status** (e.g., "Full Accreditation" or "Interim").

### 4.2 Updating Existing Records
Click on any programme row to open the **Detail View**. From here, you can update contact emails, modify dates during renewal, or "Snooze" alerts if an application is already with GTEC.

## 5.0 THE DOCUMENT VAULT

The Document Vault serves as the "Single Source of Truth" for all accreditation-related evidence.

### 5.1 Evidence Categorization
- **Accreditation Certificates**: Scanned official GTEC stamps.
- **Self-Assessment Reports (SAR)**: Draft and final versions.
- **Visitation Reports**: Feedback from GTEC assessment teams.
- **Curriculum Documents**: Latest versions reviewed by the Board of Studies.

### 5.2 Uploading Evidence
1. Navigate to the Programme's **Document Vault** section.
2. Drag and drop file (PDF, JPG, PNG).
3. Select the **Document Type** from the dropdown.
4. Add a brief note (e.g., "GTEC Certificate issued Sept 2025").

## 6.0 GTEC READINESS CHECKLIST

To streamline preparation for GTEC visitations, every programme includes a formal **Readiness Checklist**.

### 6.1 Critical Checkpoints
- [ ] Self-Assessment Report (SAR) Drafted
- [ ] New/Revised Curriculum Uploaded
- [ ] Infrastructure/Equipment Assessment Verified
- [ ] Staff Credentials & CVs Updated
- [ ] Internal QA Approval Obtained
- [ ] Official Application Submitted to GTEC

---

## 7.0 AUTOMATED NOTIFICATIONS

The system generates automated email alerts to the "Contact Email" field associated with each programme.

- **1 Year Interval**: Early warning for preparation.
- **6 Month Interval**: Final renewal submission window.
- **1 Month/Weekly**: Urgent status for critical records.
- **Daily**: Sent during the final 7 days if no renewal is recorded.
